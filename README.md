Procurement Compare

Put RFx quotes on a single comparable basis, and surface the negotiation leverage each one exposes.

Why

Three vendors answer the same RFP and return three quotes that cannot be compared. One prices per seat, one per tier with an overage cliff, one bundles implementation into year one and re-prices it in year two. The headline numbers are the least informative part of the document, and the differences that matter are buried in how each offer is structured.

Most buyers rebuild that comparison by hand in Excel, every time, and the spreadsheet is unreadable six months later.

What it does
Normalise — pulls quotes onto one basis: unit, term, ramp, what's actually included
Total cost over term — not year one; includes uplift clauses and anything that gets re-priced later
Structural diff — where offers differ in shape rather than in price, which is usually where the money is
Negotiation inputs — the leverage each quote exposes: soft terms, unusual commitments, clauses that move cheaply
